Luxury clothing

Luxora

A story of elegance.

  • Guidelines

The problem
A new luxury clothing label needed a mark and a type voice that feel inherited rather than invented, from a woven label to a storefront.
What I solved
A needle and thread emblem over a serif wordmark, a forest green and ivory palette, a two-typeface system, and guidelines that hold it together.

Logo and emblem

A needle crossed by a flowing thread: the precision of tailoring and the human hand, set above a serif wordmark.

Brand guidelines

Construction, clear space, sizing and misuse, a two-colour palette used 70 to 30, and a primary and supporting typeface with their hierarchy.

Labels and packaging

Woven labels, hang tags, a shopping bag, a gift box sealed in wax and dust bags, each carrying the emblem in the same green and ivory.

Retail and campaign

The identity at full scale: storefront, store interior, embroidered apparel and the campaign.

Beverage, fermented drinks

TÔSH

Alive hydration.

The problem
A new fermented kanji drink had to read as a modern hydration brand, with three flavours that still feel like one product.
What I solved
One label system with a per-flavour ingredient mark, reels that each sell a different reason to drink it, and a brochure that talks to retail buyers.

Packaging

A label system for the Kanji range: one structure, one type hierarchy, and an illustrated ingredient mark that changes with every flavour.

Reels

Four vertical reels, four approaches: a brand story shot on location, a battery that drains and recharges, an ingredient build, and a short story that ends on a sip of home.

Brochure and spreads

Built for retail buyers: an editorial serif against product photography, with the type doing the retail detail.

Social

Lifestyle and launch posts: the drink in everyday moments, then the three flavours side by side.

03

Bakery, hospitality

House of Cakes

Every detail crafted with elegance.

  • Identity
  • Guidelines
  • #fbbdc1
  • #462520
  • #ffe9ca
  • #d7998d
The problem
A bakery needed an identity that stays premium after it leaves the designer's hands.
What I solved
A cupcake seal that doubles as decoration, plus the construction, misuse, type and colour rules that keep it intact.

Logo system

A cupcake mark set in a scalloped seal; its flowing upper form doubles as cake decoration. Concepts and the approved variants.

Construction and limits

Clear space, proportion and misuse rules: the unglamorous pages that decide whether an identity survives other people.

Type and colour

Darker Grotesque for the wordmark, and a palette drawn from soft ivory, cocoa brown and strawberry blush.

Pharmaceuticals

HRV Pharma

The problem
A virtual pharma company, with no factories of its own, had to look credible at scale to a regulatory and clinical audience.
What I solved
A launch film for CPHI Milan, product list covers, restrained LinkedIn work, and one card system that gives HRV and NH Global Pharma two voices.

CPHI Milan announcement

A 42 second motion piece announcing HRV Global Life Sciences at CPHI Milan, 6 to 8 October 2026: Milan landmarks, kinetic type and the stand details, cut to one rhythm.

Print and collateral

API product list covers for a virtual pharma company whose proposition is scale without factories.

Social

LinkedIn work for a professional feed. Observance days handled with restraint, for an audience that is regulatory and clinical.

Pocket cards

Trade-show cards for both sides of the business: HRV states the proposition, NH Global Pharma states the reach. One system, two voices.
